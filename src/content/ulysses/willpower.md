We talk about willpower as if it were character — something you either have or lack, a verdict on the kind of person you are. It behaves much more like a budget: finite, spent through the day on a thousand small refusals, and emptiest exactly when the day has asked the most of you.

This is why the supermarket puts the sweets at the till and the streaming service queues the next episode for the hour you are least able to say no. Nobody is testing your virtue at 11pm. They are reading your balance, and they know it is low.

> You do not need more willpower. You need fewer moments that require it.

The people who look most disciplined are usually just the best at design. They remove the decision before the budget runs dry — the snacks never enter the house, the phone charges in another room. Discipline, done well, is mostly the art of not needing any.
