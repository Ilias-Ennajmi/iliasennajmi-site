Signing up took thirty seconds and one tap. Cancelling requires a phone call, during business hours, in a timezone that is not yours. This asymmetry is not incompetence. It is the product working exactly as specified — easy in, expensive out.

Friction is a lever, and most companies pull it in only one direction. They sand every edge off the path toward your money and quietly pour gravel on the path away from it. In the short term the retention numbers look wonderful. The customers are not retained; they are detained.

> Every obstacle you place between a customer and the exit is a deposit in a resentment account that compounds.

The bill comes later, in the one channel you cannot design: what people say about you when you are not in the room. Friction you add to trap someone is borrowed goodwill, at a punishing rate of interest.
